About us

We are students at the Faculty of Science and Technology of UADER, Concepción del Uruguay campus, and we develop Atria and DAV as part of a Territorial Educational Practice (PET). Both apps share the goal of offering an accessible alternative for people with reduced arm mobility, allowing them to interact with digital tools through voice commands.
